Privacy Policy for Fruidoku

Last Updated: November 19, 2026
We believe in keeping things simple and transparent. This privacy policy explains how our game works and what happens with your information.

What is our game

Fruidoku is a Sudoku game that replaces numbers with fruit images but keeps all other rules the same.

Advertisements

Our game displays advertisements to keep it free for everyone to use. These ads are provided by third-party advertising partners. While we show ads, we do not collect or share any personal information about you with advertisers.

Your Privacy Matters

We want to be crystal clear: we do not collect, store, or share any personal data about you. This includes:
  • No names or contact information
  • No email addresses
  • No location data
  • No browsing history
  • No usage statistics
  • No personal information of any kind
The game works entirely on your device. Your game sessions, scores, and progress stay on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Children's Privacy

Our game is safe for children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 or anyone else. Parents can feel confident that their children's privacy is fully protected.

Third-Party Advertising

The advertisements shown in our app are managed by third-party ad networks. These ad providers may use their own technologies to serve ads, but we do not provide them with any information about you.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this privacy policy, we will update this page and change the "Last Updated" date above. We recommend checking this page occasionally.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our app, please contact us at little.cherries@outlook.com

Your Trust

Your privacy and trust are important to us. We've designed Fruidoku to be a simple, safe, and private way to play a game without any data collection or privacy concerns.
